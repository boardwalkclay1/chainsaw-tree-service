Professional Tree Services

Elite climbing • Safe removals • Real precision

Chainsaw Clay delivers top‑tier tree services backed by real climbing skill. Every job is performed with controlled rigging, safe movement, and canopy‑level precision. No bucket trucks unless absolutely required — Clay climbs.

Tree Removal

Clay specializes in technical removals, hazardous trees, storm‑damaged structures, and tight‑space drops. Every removal is done safely from the top down using ropes, friction devices, controlled lowering, and advanced climbing technique.

Whether the tree is leaning over your home, split from a storm, or growing dangerously close to power lines, Clay handles it with precision and safety. No shortcuts. No unsafe drops.

Tree Pruning

Clay prunes trees with proper climbing technique — no spikes unless the tree is being removed. Your trees stay healthy, balanced, and shaped correctly. Clay removes deadwood, improves structure, reduces weight, and enhances canopy flow.

Proper pruning prevents future breakage, improves sunlight penetration, and increases the long‑term health of your trees.

Storm Cleanup

Storms can cause dangerous leaners, roof impacts, broken limbs, and uprooted trees. Clay responds fast and handles storm damage with controlled rigging and safe removal techniques.

Emergency service is available for trees threatening homes, vehicles, or property structures.

General Cleanup

Clay provides brush removal, debris clearing, yard cleanup, and property restoration after storms or overgrowth. Whether your yard needs a full reset or just a cleanup, Clay handles it quickly and professionally.

Planting & Layout

Clay helps design and plant new trees with proper spacing, soil preparation, and long‑term growth planning. Planting trees correctly ensures healthy development and prevents future structural issues.
