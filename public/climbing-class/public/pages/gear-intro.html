Understanding Your Equipment Can Save Your Life

Before anyone becomes a great climber, they must become a safe climber. Every professional arborist, recreational tree climber, rope access technician, and aerial professional depends on their equipment every single day.

This course teaches you how to inspect, maintain, select, understand, and properly use climbing equipment before ever leaving the ground.

What You'll Learn

Harness Systems

  • Harness Types
  • Proper Fitment
  • Bridge Inspection
  • Adjustment Methods

Climbing Ropes

  • Rope Construction
  • Inspection Procedures
  • Wear Identification
  • Retirement Guidelines

Carabiners

  • Gate Types
  • Strength Ratings
  • Inspection Procedures
  • Safe Usage

Safety Systems

  • Redundancy
  • Backup Systems
  • Work Positioning
  • Risk Assessment

Why This Course Matters

Many climbing accidents occur because equipment was either used improperly, inspected incorrectly, or misunderstood. Understanding your gear creates a foundation that improves every other skill taught throughout the academy. Without strong equipment knowledge, advanced climbing techniques become significantly more dangerous.
