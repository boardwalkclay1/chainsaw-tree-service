Your Personal Tree‑Climbing Guide — Anywhere, Anytime

Chainsaw Clay’s Climbing Class is a complete climbing system built for both beginners and working arborists. Every lesson is designed to be used in the tree, on the jobsite, or during practice climbs.

You’ll learn gear setup, lifeline control, movement technique, throwball mastery, rigging, cutting, and jobsite flow.

The training app includes interactive video quizzes, step‑by‑step breakdowns, slow‑motion climbing footage, and real canopy examples.

Leisure Climbing Professional Arborist Training

Whether you climb for fun or for work, this system grows with you — from your first knot to your first 100‑foot ascent.
